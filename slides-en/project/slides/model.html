Parallel level model

When the sensor goes blind, the tank model steers, for at most 45 s

The model integrates pump in minus drain out and is pulled towards every accepted reading with gain 0.05.

Before it, fills stopped at about 50 %. After 45 s without a real reading: LEVEL_LOST, pump off.

Flow derived from level

in = 0.36 L/min · pump

out = −rate × A × 60/1000

Outflow is measured only with the pump off and settled 20 s, then held while pumping.

The old formula made the red outflow curve jump every time the pump started.

Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 11

Two uses of a simple tank model. First, it bridges short dropouts of the ultrasonic sensor during a fill, with hard limits: 45 s maximum, and a returning reading that disagrees by more than 2.5 cm is treated as suspect. Second, outflow. The old formula subtracted a lagging rate from an instantly switching pump term, so the dashboard showed consumption rising whenever the pump started. The valve sets the draw, not the pump, so we hold the drain estimate while pumping.
