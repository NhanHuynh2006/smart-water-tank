Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 10

Each gate has a rejection counter published in telemetry (rj_gate, rj_spread, rj_rate, rj_fall). That is how we found that 98 percent of rejections came from one gate, the spread check. It used max minus min, which one stray echo controls completely. Switching to the interquartile range raised acceptance from 60 to 93 percent. The alpha-beta filter tracks level and velocity; the velocity is reused for flow and pump diagnosis.
