07 · Lessons

Bugs found on the bench

FOUND BY · CURRENT RIPPLE

Inverted relay

Every stop command started the pump while telemetry said “off”. Ripple 27 mV versus 4.5 mV proved which state was real.

FOUND BY · REPLAYED HISTORY

Pump started at every boot

The level was flagged valid before any reading, at 0 %. Fixed: a restart at 33 % on 5 Oct reported 32 %, pump stayed off.

FOUND BY · STEPWISE CALIBRATION

A repeatable echo, not the water

At 7.9–8.7 cm from the sensor, 40 of 40 pings gave the same wrong value. A rule that trusted repeats started the pump every 40 s.

FOUND BY · BROKER LOG

Silent truncation

JSON cut at the buffer and an exception swallowed by the callback: everything green, nothing stored.
