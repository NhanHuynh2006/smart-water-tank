01 · The problem

What a smart tank must do

MEASURE

Level and flow, checked against a physical reference

CONTROL

Hold the level in a band without the pump chattering

PROTECT

Never overflow, even with a bad sensor or a manual command

DIAGNOSE

Tell a dry pump from a dead pump; spot abnormal use

SUPERVISE

History, daily volume and alarms, from anywhere

SURVIVE

Keep controlling the tank when the network is gone
