Manual mode cannot bypass safety because there is no other code path: auto and manual both call startPump, which calls the guard. Reviewing safety means reviewing one function. In the demo we will start the pump by hand and lift the upper float: it stops within one cycle, and clearing the fault is refused while the float is still up. The layers on the right are independent: the raw distance guard, the time caps and the float do not depend on the filtered level.
