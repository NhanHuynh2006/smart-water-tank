We think pointing precisely at what falls short is as valuable as showing what works; this is Section 5.7 of the report. The main open item is flow measurement, highlighted. The hardware is fitted and the inlet now counts water and is calibrated, but we have validated it on one calibration run, so control and the volume counter still use flow from the level. The next step is to validate it over complete fills and move the volume counter to it, and to replace the outlet sensor with one rated down to 0.05 litres per minute. Second, the current scale factor: we read 471 to 487 milliamps for a pump rated 100 to 200, so either the divider or the sensor variant differs from our assumption; one multimeter reading settles it, and the present-or-absent use of current is unaffected. Third, the no current rule has not been triggered by a physical injection; we will pull a pump wire in the demo. Fourth, the ultrasonic blind zone: foam around the transducer, a stilling tube or a higher bracket would remove the stray echo. The rolling baseline needs several days to converge. And for a real deployment: MQTTS on port 8883, because the lab broker is not encrypted, a named tunnel, per-user accounts and over-the-air updates.
