Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 27

The flow measurement is the main open item. The fix is cheap and known. A real pulse at 0.36 L/min is about 14 ms long, while the conducted interference is well under a millisecond, so a minimum pulse width filter in the interrupt would separate them, and a supply that does not share a return with the motor would remove the cause. The binary use of current, present or absent, is unaffected by the scale question.
