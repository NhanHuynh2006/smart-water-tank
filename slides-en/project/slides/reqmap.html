This table takes each requirement of the brief, says how we meet it, and gives an honest status; it is the same as Table 1.3.1 in the report. Most rows are met and demonstrated. Two points I want to be upfront about. First, the flow sensors: both are fitted, wired and read, and since the rewiring of the fifth of October the inlet sensor counts real water and is calibrated against the ruler. But control and the volume counter still use flow derived from the level, and the outlet sensor is below its starting flow at our drain rates, so we call this requirement partly validated, not simply met. Second, the advanced component: we built both options the brief offers, a rolling baseline for abnormal consumption and a classifier based on pump current versus flow. The classifier works and we will demo it; the baseline needs three days of data per half hour slot before it may alarm, and our bench days do not reach that yet.
