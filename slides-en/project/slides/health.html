This is the second advanced option of the brief. The classifier is diagnostic, not protective: NO_CURRENT and NO_PROGRESS stop the pump. Its value is telling the operator whether to check the wiring or the plumbing. The dashboard shows the label under the current tile and in a column of the fault table. In the demo we lift the suction out of the water and watch it change to no_flow.
