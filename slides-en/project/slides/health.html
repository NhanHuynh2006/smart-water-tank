The brief offers two advanced options and we built both. The first is classifying pump faults by comparing pump current with water movement, and this slide shows the four cases. Normal current with the water moving means a healthy pump. Low current, about 220 milliamps instead of about 325, means the pump is running dry: the intake is out of the water and the motor spins without load, so we stop it within about five seconds. Normal current but no water moving after 25 seconds is labelled no_flow: a hydraulic problem after the pump, such as a blocked or detached pipe. And no current at all is labelled no_current: an electrical problem, such as a broken wire, a bad relay or a dead motor. So the same symptom, a tank that does not fill, now points the technician to the right place. The label is saved with every fault, and in the demo we will lift the intake out of the water to show the dry case.
