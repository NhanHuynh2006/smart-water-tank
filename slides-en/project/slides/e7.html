Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 24

Two lessons. First, a one-way delay subtracts two clocks, and NTP on a microcontroller is only good to tens of milliseconds; after one reboot we saw 1100 ms one-way while the round trip was 187 ms. Second, the ESP32 Arduino core enables modem sleep by default; turning it off cut the tail by two thirds at the cost of roughly double radio current, fine for a mains powered device. The final session also used a different access point, so we do not attribute the last drop to one cause.
