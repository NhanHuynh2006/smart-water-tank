Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 12

Seven states. The hysteresis band is 30 to 70 percent with 15 percent margin to the overflow limit. Why a state machine instead of an if statement: fault states must be sticky, otherwise a pump that sucked air restarts the moment the tube touches water again. One invariant is enforced every cycle: if a fault code is set, the state must be a fault state. An early version broke this and looked idle while refusing every start.
