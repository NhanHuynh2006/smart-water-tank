Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 13

The brief's example, pump on with near zero flow, is DRY_RUN on the turbine signal. With flow derived from level it is disabled, because a constant can never fall below a threshold, and a guard that cannot fire is worse than none. NO_PROGRESS carries the same physical idea on a measured quantity: current flows but the level does not rise. The 45 s sensor timeout is long on purpose: the sensor drops out for tens of seconds during normal fills and the model covers that; with a 4 s timeout the first version stopped every fill early.
