04 · Fault detection

Eleven fault rules

CodeTriggerAction
SENSOR_TIMEOUTNo accepted level for 45 s, 90 s with the modelSelf-clears
SENSOR_CONFLICTA float disagrees with the ultrasonic levelSelf-clears
LEVEL_LOSTFilling blind on the model for over 90 sSelf-clears
OVERFLOWUpper float, raw < 4.5 cm, or level ≥ 85 %Lock
NO_PROGRESSPump on 90 s, level up by less than 1.5 cmLock
NO_CURRENTRelay closed, no pump current for 2 sLock
DRY_RUNTurbine flow under 0.25 L/min for 6 sLock
FILL_TIMEOUTOne run longer than 240 sLock
HARD_LIMITOne run longer than 300 s, unconditionallyLock
VOLUME_LIMITMore than 2.5 L pumped in one runLock
LEAK_SUSPECTEDPump off, inflow over 0.2 L/min for 60 sWarn
The brief asks for one fault rule; we have eleven, checked every 200 ms, and any rule that fires stops the pump at once. OVERFLOW has three independent triggers: the float, the raw distance and the filtered level. NO_PROGRESS is the brief's example rule, pump on with no flow, measured by the level not rising. The sensor timeout is long on purpose, 90 seconds while the model is valid, because shorter limits stopped normal fills.
