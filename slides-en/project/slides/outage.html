Network outage

Lose the network, keep the tank

Loop never waits

Socket open capped at 0.5 s (default 3 s), CONNACK at 2 s. Longest control period reported as ctl_gap_ms.

Nothing forgotten

240-sample ring buffer, 4 min at 1 Hz, replayed with “replay”: true. Volume saved to flash every 60 s.

Everyone knows

Broker publishes the last will after the 15 s keep alive; the dashboard turns red after 5 s of silence.

Finds its way back

Backoff 1 to 30 s, up to 3 Wi-Fi networks, broker found again by mDNS name.

Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 20

The course warns against connection logic inside a blocking loop. Our reconnection is non blocking, but opening a TCP socket to an unreachable broker still blocks inside the stack, three seconds by default on the ESP32. We cap it at half a second and publish the longest control period in the first message after reconnecting, so the claim that control never stalls can be checked, not just asserted.
