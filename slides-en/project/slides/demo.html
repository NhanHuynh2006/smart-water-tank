Six things you will see on the real tank

  1. Open the drain: level falls on the projector and on a phone over 4G
  2. Below the low band the pump starts by itself, and stops at 70 %
  3. Manual command: “sending”, then the device's confirmed answer
  4. Manual pump on, lift the upper float: it stops, clearing is refused
  5. Lift the suction out of the water: health turns to no_flow
  6. Switch the hotspot off mid-fill: the pump still stops at 70 %, and the gap refills on reconnect
