Fault detection. These rows come from the fault, pump event and command logs of the twenty first to twenty third of September. Unplugging the echo wire raised SENSOR_TIMEOUT forty to forty one seconds after the last trusted message, which matches the forty five second design of that firmware once you account for the five seconds before a reading is marked untrusted. The final firmware lengthens this to ninety seconds while the tank model is valid, because crossing the blind zone takes up to seventy five seconds; that value has not been re-timed. In all eighteen overflow and no progress events where the pump was running, the pump off message went out in the same two hundred millisecond cycle as the fault. Every manual start during a fault was refused with the right reason. We are upfront about the orange row: we never pulled a pump wire to trigger NO_CURRENT, so its two seconds is a design value, not a measurement. What we did measure is the signal it depends on: with the relay closed the current read 471 to 487 milliamps in every message, and zero after it opened, a margin of about eleven times the noise floor. And NO_PROGRESS fired twice for real on the fifth of October: the drain valve let out as much as the pump delivered at forty eight percent, the level hovered for ninety seconds, and the rule stopped the pump with the health label no_flow. We will pull a pump wire live in the demo.
