Architecture

Four blocks in series, one loop at the edge

Perception: ESP32 and sensors. Transport: Mosquitto over Wi-Fi. Processing: FastAPI and SQLite. Application: the dashboard.

A Cloudflare tunnel publishes the dashboard over HTTPS, without opening an inbound port.

Walk the diagram top to bottom. Physical process at the top, the ESP32 in the middle running the closed loop every 200 ms, the laptop at the bottom with broker, backend, database and dashboard. Point out that the only arrow from the server to the pump goes through the ESP32's own checks: the server can request, never force.
