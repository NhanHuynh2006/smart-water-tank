Project 08 · Smart Water Tank · 6

We measured the pump instead of trusting the datasheet: 1.2 cm rise in 20 s with the outlet closed gives 0.36 L/min, almost five times below the rated 1.67 because of head and tubing. Every timing threshold in the firmware is sized from that measured number. A small tank makes a full fill and drain cycle last minutes, so we could observe dozens of cycles per session.
