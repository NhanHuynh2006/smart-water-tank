% ======== Abstract ========
\begin{abstract}
\sloppy

This project builds a closed loop smart water tank for Project 08: it measures water level and flow, drives a pump automatically, accumulates consumed volume, detects and classifies faults, and can be supervised from anywhere on the Internet. The system spans the layered model of the Internet of Things: an ESP32 with an ultrasonic level sensor, two turbine flow sensors, a Hall current sensor and two float switches; MQTT over Wi-Fi to a Mosquitto broker; a FastAPI service with a SQLite time series store; and a web dashboard published through an HTTPS tunnel, where anyone may watch and only a password authenticated administrator may control.

The central design decision is to place the entire control law and every safety rule inside the edge microcontroller. Level regulation therefore keeps working while the network is down, and a ring buffer replays telemetry when it returns. The controller is a seven state machine with a 30 to 70 percent hysteresis band and minimum on and off times; eleven fault rules, a single guard function through which every pump start must pass, and independent overflow layers ensure that manual commands cannot bypass safety. Level readings pass a robust filter chain (median window, interquartile spread gate, physical rate checks and an alpha-beta filter), and a pump health classifier separates hydraulic from electrical pump faults using current and level evidence. A rolling baseline over 48 half hour slots detects abnormal consumption on the backend.

On the bench, every automatic fill stopped between 70.1 and 70.8 percent with at most 1.85 percent overshoot, the pump started 12.9 times per hour with no relay chatter, no false alarm was raised in 84 minutes, pumped volume was estimated with a mean error of $-5.2$ percent against a geometric reference, and commands were confirmed with a median round trip of 42 milliseconds. The report states openly where the prototype falls short, chiefly that conducted interference from the pump makes the turbine flow sensors unusable in this installation, so flow is derived from the level.

\end{abstract}
